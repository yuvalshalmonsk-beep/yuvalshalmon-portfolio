About Me

I'm Yuval Shalmon, a brand and digital designer based in Tel Aviv, with roots in a kibbutz in northern Israel.

I've always been drawn to contrasts. Nature and technology, instinct and research, bold ideas and the smallest details. I don't believe in choosing one over the other. More often than not, the interesting stuff happens somewhere in between.

I'm interested in what makes people and brands distinct. The things that aren't always written in a brief, but make all the difference. I like to look beyond the obvious, ask the right questions, and build visual identities that have something to say - not just something to show.
