Kanopy

Brand IdentityIllustrationWeb DesignUX/UIConcept

Info

A bold cybersecurity brand built for the wild world of no-code and AI-powered business apps. Through a hand-crafted illustration system, vibrant identity, and digital experience, we positioned Kanopy as the guide that brings clarity and order to the jungle.

Role

Lead Designer - Brand Identity & Web
Created at Guts Agency

Year

2025

Site

kanopysecurity.com

The jungle became a metaphor for the world Kanopy operates in constantly growing, full of possibility, and increasingly difficult to see through. Rather than framing innovation as the threat, the brand positions Kanopy as the guide that brings visibility, clarity, and order to the wild.

I developed the visual world from scratch, creating the logo, illustration system, plants, animals, objects, and icons by hand. This human-made approach gave the brand a distinctive character in a category increasingly filled with generic, synthetic visuals.

Fortune favors the bold.
