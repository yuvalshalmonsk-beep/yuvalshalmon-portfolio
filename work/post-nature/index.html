Post Nature

Post Nature is a speculative interactive exhibition exploring human intervention in the plant world. Through a physical installation and two digital interfaces, it invites visitors to examine how plants have changed over time and imagine what we might turn them into next. Graduation project, HIT. Mentor: Bob Orel.
