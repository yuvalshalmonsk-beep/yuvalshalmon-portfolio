Info

Act is a cloud security company built on the belief that lasting security starts at the foundation. Founded by a team with decades of experience in security and critical infrastructure, Act addresses cloud risks at their source rather than simply responding to threats.

Inspired by Act’s core strategy of strong foundations, we turned to the Roman Empire.

As part of my work at Guts Agency, I developed a visual language imagining Act in ancient Rome. Through monumental, impossible structures built on solid foundations, classical sculptures, and contemporary aesthetics, I created a brand universe combining strength, stability, imagination, and innovation.
